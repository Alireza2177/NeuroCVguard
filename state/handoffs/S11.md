Implemented REQ-S11 and acceptance cases AT-S11-01 through AT-S11-09. The user
explicitly requested S11. Completed S10 outputs and their recorded passing checks
were used as prerequisites; no human acceptance or scientific review was inferred.
S12 and later stages remain NOT_STARTED. No publication or remote operation occurred.

New `_inner_plans.py` derives missing inner assignments from outer training only
using the existing participant-level grouped planner. Supplied valid inner folds
are retained, including partially supplied plans; invalid assignments are refused.
Both supplied and generated memberships undergo independent auditing before fits.
Participant identity and transitive protected components remain indivisible.
Site/phase outer objectives retain their domain constraints; inner selection has
the explicitly documented unseen-participant objective.

New `_tuning.py` implements a small deterministic C loop. Every candidate uses the
same memberships and fresh estimators. The objective is pooled inner participant
balanced accuracy after averaging each participant's observation probabilities.
Scores within 1e-12 of the maximum tie; the smallest numeric C wins. All candidate
scores and selected C are retained, together with the selection policy. A required
inner failure leaves its candidate score null with a reason and prevents outer
refitting, even when another candidate succeeds. All candidates are evaluated.

Extended `_evaluation_inputs.py`, `_evaluation_fits.py` and `evaluation.py` for
validated inner plans, observed inner fit events and fresh selected-C refits on
all outer training rows. The original supplied plan is unchanged. The private
result retains the derived plan and digest using the fields already approved in
S10; no additional schema or normative change was needed. Fixed-C mode performs
no inner fitting or selection. Failed folds remain visible and prevent a complete
pooled score.

Updated root/CLI descriptions, the standalone planner's scope message and the
safe public evaluation limitations. Public reports omit candidate scores and
identity-bearing plans/fit events. Updated README.md, docs/cli.md,
docs/evaluation.md and AI_ASSISTANCE.md. Added tests/test_inner_plans.py,
tests/test_nested_candidates.py, tests/test_nested_runner.py and
